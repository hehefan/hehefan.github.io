BIO

I am currently a ZJU100 Young Professor with the College of Artificial Intelligence at Zhejiang University. Before that, I was a Research Fellow with Prof. Mohan S. Kankanhalli at National University of Singapore. My research focuses on computer vision, LLM & agent, embodied AI and AI for science. I received my PhD degree at University of Technology Sydney, advised by Prof. Yi Yang.

Recruiting

招收博士研究生、硕士研究生、博士后研究员、助理研究员。

为便于更好地规划科研方向，建议有意申请博士和硕士研究生的同学，在大三开始之前联系。

我们会优先考虑在ACM ICPC、NOI等编程竞赛中有所历练，或已积累了一定的学术成果（TPAMI、IJCV、NeurIPS、ICLR、ICML、CVPR、ICCV、ECCV、ACL、EMNLP、ICRA、IROS、CoRL），或C9前10%（纯课程排名）的同学。

也欢迎控制、药学和材料专业的同学加入！
